Frequently Asked Questions

Many issues can be resolved without raising a ticket. Search below, or browse the most common questions we receive.

Results filter as you type.

Use the self-service password reset portal on the intranet homepage and authenticate with your registered mobile number. If your account is locked after five failed attempts, it unlocks automatically after 15 minutes, or you can call the hotline to have it released sooner. Service Desk staff will never ask you for your current or new password at any point in the process.

Response times are governed by the priority you select: one hour for Critical, four hours for High, one business day for Medium and three business days for Low. Resolution time depends on the nature of the issue — most password and access requests close the same day, while hardware replacements may take longer. You will receive an email at every status change, and you can check progress at any time in the self-service portal.

Open the VPN client pinned to your taskbar, choose the Corporate-SG profile and sign in with your network account plus the code from your authenticator app. If the client reports a certificate error, disconnect from any hotel or captive-portal Wi-Fi first and reconnect once you have normal internet access. Persistent failures are usually an expired device certificate — raise a Network & VPN ticket and include the error code shown in the client.

Disconnect the docking station and all peripherals, hold the power button for 15 seconds, then connect the charger directly and power on again. If you see a blue recovery screen or the machine cycles without reaching the login prompt, stop and do not attempt a factory reset — that risks your locally cached files. Call the hotline for a Priority High ticket, and mention whether the charging light is on so we can bring a loan device if needed.

Check the Software Centre on your laptop first — anything pre-approved for your role installs on demand with no ticket required. For titles not in the catalogue, raise a Software / Application ticket with the business justification; your line manager approves it and IT Security reviews the licence and risk assessment. Please do not download or install software from the internet yourself, as unapproved installs are blocked by policy.

Do not click any link, open any attachment, or reply to the sender. Use the Report Phishing button in your mail client — this forwards the message safely to IT Security and removes it from your inbox. Never forward a suspicious email to colleagues, and if you have already clicked a link or entered your credentials, call the hotline immediately and raise a Critical Security Incident ticket.

Raise an Account & Access ticket naming the exact drive path or application, and state whether you need read or read-write permission. Requests route to your line manager and then to the data owner for approval, so include the business reason to avoid delays. Access is granted to your own account only — sharing credentials or working under a colleague's login is a policy breach and will be flagged during access reviews.

Sign in to the self-service portal and open My Tickets to see every request raised under your staff ID with its current status and assigned team. You can add notes, attach screenshots or ask for a priority change directly on the ticket, which alerts the engineer straight away. Quoting your reference number — for example UOB-ITSD-20250131-4821 — will also get you to the right person faster when you call the hotline.
